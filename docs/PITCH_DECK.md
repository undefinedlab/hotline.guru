## Design direction (for the slide AI)

- **Look:** liquid glass / frosted panels on near-black (`#070707`)
- **Accent:** sharp yellow (`#F5E000`) — sparingly, for emphasis and CTAs
- **Type:** clean sans (Manrope / similar). Bold headlines, not cursive, not poster-fat
- **Layout:** one idea per slide; left copy / right visual ok; avoid dense walls of text
- **Motion (if web/deck supports it):** subtle parallax, soft glow, restrained — professional not gimmicky
- **Avoid:** purple AI gradients, cluttered dashboards, emoji, multi-card hero spam

## Slide outline (recommended: 9 slides)

### Slide 1 — Title

**Headline:** Your phone number is the wallet.  
**Subhead:** hotline.guru — the phone-native OS for agentic USDC.  
**Supporting:** Call your money. It moves under policy. It buys the world.  
**Footer meta:** Circle Arc · Agentic economy · Voice-first · 2026  

**Visual cue:** Phone / dial tone / signal motif in glass panel. Chips: Voice · SMS · Arc USDC · x402.

---

### Slide 2 — Problem

**Headline:** Settlement works. The last mile doesn’t.  
**Body:**
- Billions can dial a number. Almost none will install MetaMask, pass KYC theater, or reason about gas.
- Banks need paperwork. Wallets need folklore.
- Chatty AI spenders without hard limits are a liability — not a product.

**Stats (optional callouts):**
- **2.5B+** feature-phone users who can dial, not DeFi
- **$T** remittance still on cash & airtime
- **0** apps required for the end user

**Visual cue:** Friction stack — Seed / KYC / Gas = FAIL · Dial a number = PASS.

---

### Slide 3 — Solution / Product

**Headline:** A money agent you call.  
**Quote block:**
> Call or text your agent. It spends USDC on Arc under a deterministic policy gate.  
> — No bank · No app · No Uniswap

**Pillars:**
1. Telephony = UI  
2. Policy = leash  
3. Arc USDC = money  
4. x402 = the world  

**Architecture strip (thin):**  
Voice / SMS ingress → Intent + policy gate → Circle / Arc wallets → x402 marketplace  

**Principle:** LLM proposes. Code disposes.

---

### Slide 4 — How it works (actionable flow)

**Headline:** Sixty seconds to settled.  

| Step | Title | Copy |
|------|--------|------|
| 01 | Dial | Your number is the account. Login is a ringtone. |
| 02 | Speak | “Send five dollars to Bob.” Names resolve — not hex. |
| 03 | Policy | Soft caps, daily limits, hard ceilings. Over-limit = refuse. |
| 04 | Settle + buy | USDC on Arc. Or nanopay an x402 service for answers. |

**Punch line:** P2P is table stakes. Buying the world is the product.  
**CTA on slide:** “Book a live demo” / “Run the 60s path”

---

### Slide 5 — Trust / Moat

**Headline:** Policy before personality.  
**Body:** Competitors demo chatty spenders. We demo an agent that **cannot** overspend. Institutions need a leash — not a vibe.

**Three cards:**
1. **Gate — Deterministic limits**  
   Per-tx soft cap · daily budget · hard ceiling · nanopay cap
2. **Moat — Own the inbound**  
   Phone ↔ wallet identity graph. Circle for money. We own the dial tone.
3. **Expand — Buy the world**  
   x402 turns the hotline into a purchasing agent (prices, fraud, research, APIs).

## What we ship vs what we refuse (optional contrast slide)

**Ship**
- USDC only  
- Voice as primary UI  
- Hard policy over soft vibes  
- Human-readable payees (phone, contacts, names)  
- Marketplace via x402  

**Refuse**
- DEX / custom token theater  
- Multi-chain bridge UIs  
- Perps / prediction / insurance scope creep  
- LLM-authorized spending  
- App-store dependency for the end user
